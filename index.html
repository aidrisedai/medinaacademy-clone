Top Cognia Accredited Islamic School in WA State

As a mark of academic excellence, Cognia accreditation shows our school has met and exceeded rigorous standards in education, governance, and leadership. This ongoing, meticulous process ensures we consistently measure and improve our programs over time, demonstrating our unwavering commitment to providing a high-quality education.

Council Of Islamic Schools North America Accredited

As the gold standard for Islamic education, CISNA accreditation certifies that our school upholds the highest standards for academics and Islamic education. This ensures our school culture is holistically integrated with the values of Islam, setting us a step above the rest.
